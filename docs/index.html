FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without signing in.

What modes are in the app?

Four- and six-button quizzes, a spelling (letters) mode, and a browsable country table. Pick what fits your study style.

In-app purchases and ads

The app may offer in-app purchases to remove ads or unlock extra content, depending on your build and store settings. You can manage purchases in your App Store account settings.

Privacy

We publish a dedicated Privacy policy for the app, including how advertising (such as Google AdMob) and App Store purchases work. Use the same link in App Store Connect’s Privacy Policy URL.

App won’t start or keeps crashing

Make sure you are on a supported iOS version, restart the device, and update the app from the App Store. If the issue persists, email with your device model and iOS version.
